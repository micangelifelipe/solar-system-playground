    🌌 Welcome to Solar System LAB

    Explore, experiment and discover the universe

    Solar System LAB is an interactive space created for education and science outreach, where you can explore the Solar System, interact with celestial bodies and experiment with different astronomical phenomena.

    The idea is to learn through curiosity, observation and experimentation, in a fun way that is accessible to everyone.

    The universe is in your hands! Dare to experiment.

    ⚠ An important note: this is an educational simulation. Some scales, motions, interactions and physical phenomena have been simplified to make them easier to understand. Therefore, the results should not be interpreted as exact scientific representations or predictions.
